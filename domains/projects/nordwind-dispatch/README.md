# Nordwind dispatch

> **Demo content. The customer is invented and every fact below with it.**

## What we are doing

Nordwind Logistik GmbH plans next-day deliveries in a shared spreadsheet.
One dispatcher rebuilds the plan by hand every afternoon, and it is out of date by the time the first driver reads it.

We are in discovery, working out whether a planning service is the right answer and what it would have to do.
Nothing has been quoted and nothing has been committed.

## What discovery has to settle

- Whether the existing ERP can export the day's orders in a usable shape, or whether that is a project of its own.
- Whether the dispatcher's manual overrides are rules that can be written down, or judgement that cannot.
- What "good enough" means for a route plan here, in the dispatcher's own terms.
- Whether this is one project or the first of several.

## What is deliberately not decided yet

Scope, budget, and delivery shape.
Discovery exists to produce the estimate, so committing to any of them now would make the workshop decorative.

See [discovery workshop](../../value-creation/discovery-workshop/README.md) for how this phase runs.
