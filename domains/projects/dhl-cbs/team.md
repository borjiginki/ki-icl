## How this file is meant to be read

Names are here because a CEO or project manager asking "who is working on this" needs to reach someone, and a role with no name attached does not answer that.

What is deliberately **not** here is anything about how an individual is performing, how much they delivered, or how their time compares to anyone else's.
Progress belongs to the project and is reported in [status.md](status.md), never to a person.

That boundary is not a style preference.
Under German law a record that lets an employer assess individual performance is personal data with a works council dimension, and building it as a side effect of status reporting would be the wrong way to arrive at it.
See `project-status-reporting` in the `value-creation` domain.
