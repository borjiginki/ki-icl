# Bosch — Product & Service Taxation Consolidation

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Industry

## At a glance

PMO-led governance for Bosch's international taxation consolidation — coordinating teams and creating transparency toward a harmonized, tax-robust architecture.

## Initial situation

Bosch C/TXB Tax Business Interface and M&A faced the strategic challenge of consolidating international product and service taxation. Country-specific regulations and fragmented structures limited transparency, controllability and tax robustness; they aimed for a harmonized, resilient target architecture.

## Approach

Assuming the PMO role, we ensured reliable governance of this complex, internationally structured project — monitoring progress and timelines, coordinating all project activities end-to-end, and managing communication between functional teams and stakeholders through defined governance and reporting structures.

## Results

- Stable foundation for a **harmonized, tax-robust target architecture**
- **Fact-based management decisions** through clear governance and decision logs
- **Transparency** on project status, dependencies and risks via structured assessments
