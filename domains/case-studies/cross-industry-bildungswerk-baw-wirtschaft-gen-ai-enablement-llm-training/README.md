# Bildungswerk BaWü Wirtschaft — Gen AI Enablement & LLM Training

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Education

## At a glance

A large-scale Gen AI enablement initiative — 700+ trained in two days as part of Microsoft's AI Skills Alliance, with a roadmap to reach 50,000 people.

## Initial situation

In 2024 Baden-Württemberg launched a Generative AI initiative to promote AI education across society and among SMEs. During the kickoff event over 700 participants were trained in AI fundamentals and tools like Microsoft Copilot within just two days.

## Approach

As part of Microsoft's AI Skills Alliance, the initiative aimed to empower 50,000 people and accelerate digital transformation across German businesses. By providing practical, tool-based training at scale, we helped enable broad AI adoption in public and private sectors.

## Results

- Participants reported **greater confidence** using AI in real-world tasks
- **Strong engagement** during training and positive post-event feedback
- **Foundation** created for further regional rollouts
- **Program now expanding** to new target groups nationwide
