# EGLV — Automated Detection of Blocking Materials

**Sector:** Power & Utilities · **Industry:** Water Management

## At a glance

A computer-vision solution for EGLV that auto-detects materials blocking waterways — cutting manual inspection effort by 80%.

## Initial situation

Emschergenossenschaft / Lippeverband (EGLV) needed to detect materials blocking waterways and infrastructure, relying on manual inspection.

## Approach

We developed an automated computer-vision solution to detect blocking materials, replacing manual inspection.

## Results

- **Automated detection** of blocking materials via computer vision
- **80% reduction** in manual inspection effort
- Faster, more reliable **infrastructure monitoring**
