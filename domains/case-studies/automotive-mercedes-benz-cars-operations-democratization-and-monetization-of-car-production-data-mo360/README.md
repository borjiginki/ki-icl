# Mercedes-Benz Cars Operations — Democratization and monetization of car production data (MO360)

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Production

## At a glance

The MO360 data platform for Mercedes-Benz production — enabling data-driven operations, digital-twin capabilities and the democratization and monetization of production data.

## Initial situation

MO360 is the central initiative for Mercedes-Benz' production ecosystem — a family of software apps seamlessly connected via shared databases and standardized interfaces to maximize efficiency, flexibility, transparency and time/cost savings during car production.

## Approach

We supported the establishment of the MO360 data platform — a game changer for digitization in MO for data-driven operations and the digital twin — enabling democratization and monetization of production data across the organization and its suppliers.

## Results

- Definition of the **data platform services and capabilities**
- Specification of the **procedural and technical data integration**
- **Technical concept** for data monetization
