# DAT Group — Voice-based Parts Selection

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Aftermarket

## At a glance

A 'Voice to Calc' system for DAT Group — Azure and OpenAI converting spoken vehicle-damage inputs into compliant calculations in under 10 seconds, shown at Automechanika 2024.

## Initial situation

DAT is a leading software provider for vehicle damage calculation. Traditionally, damage selection is done via dropdown menus on laptops or tablets; for smaller damages, a 'Voice to Calc' approach offers a faster alternative.

## Approach

Our system converts speech into actionable tasks, retrieves verified repair procedures from the DAT database and generates comprehensive work orders, synthesizing job requirements, parts and calculations. With Azure and OpenAI, the tech stack enhances operational efficiency and accuracy.

## Results

- Live or uploaded **voice inputs converted into DAT-compliant calculations in under 10 seconds**
- MVP presented to the Executive and Supervisory Boards with **strong support**
- **Showcased at Automechanika 2024** (Frankfurt)
