# BayWa r.e. — IoT Data Platform for Wind & Solar

**Sector:** Power & Utilities · **Industry:** Renewable Energy

## At a glance

An Azure Databricks IoT data platform for BayWa r.e. wind and solar assets — governed by Unity Catalog and scaled for operational analytics.

## Initial situation

BayWa r.e. needed to harness IoT data from wind and solar assets to drive operations and analytics at scale.

## Approach

We built an IoT data platform on Azure Databricks with Unity Catalog, ingesting and governing wind and solar asset data for analytics.

## Results

- **IoT data platform** for wind and solar assets
- Built on **Azure Databricks** with **Unity Catalog** governance
- Scalable foundation for **operations and analytics**
