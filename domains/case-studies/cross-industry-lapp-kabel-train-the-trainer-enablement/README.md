# Lapp Kabel — Train-the-Trainer Enablement

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Industrial Technology

## At a glance

A Train-the-Trainer Copilot program with live demos and prompt labs, empowering 20+ internal trainers and a reusable toolkit for company-wide scale-out.

## Initial situation

LAPP, a global provider of cable and connection solutions, is committed to AI-driven transformation. Beyond the initial Copilot rollout, internal trainers needed to confidently guide everyday users — demystifying Copilot, building prompt literacy and setting realistic expectations.

## Approach

We designed and delivered a Train-the-Trainer program combining live demos, use case simulations and prompt labs, focused on real-world application with LAPP-relevant documents, emails and scenarios. Trainers received a reusable enablement toolkit with slides, prompt libraries, videos, articles and FAQs.

## Results

- **Empowered 20+ trainers** with hands-on M365 experience
- **Tailored toolkit** for scale-out across regions and departments
- **Lasting enablement assets** that reduce IT dependency and accelerate adoption
- Shifted mindset from explaining Copilot to **enabling self-service**
