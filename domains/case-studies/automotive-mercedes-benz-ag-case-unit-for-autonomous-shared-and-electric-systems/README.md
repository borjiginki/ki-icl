# Mercedes-Benz AG — CASE Unit for autonomous, shared and electric systems

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Mobility

## At a glance

A Services & Ecosystem strategy for Mercedes-Benz CASE — governance, the first AI & Analytics Competence Center and global tech/start-up/open-source collaborations.

## Initial situation

The client needed to accelerate innovation in the rapidly evolving mobility sector. Internal innovation alone was insufficient; a structured approach to engaging external ecosystems, start-ups and technology partners was required for sustainable competitiveness.

## Approach

We conceived and executed a scalable Services & Ecosystem strategy for future mobility, including a governance and steering model for ecosystem interaction, the creation of an AI & Analytics Competence Center, and structured collaborations with global tech, start-up and open-source communities.

## Results

- Established a **clear governance model** for ecosystem engagement
- Launched the **first company-wide AI & Analytics Competence Center**
- Connected the organization to **global tech and start-up ecosystems**
- Built collaborations with **leading open-source communities**
- Positioned the client as an **agile, innovation-driven leader**
