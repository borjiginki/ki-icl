# Daimler Truck — Creation of tb.lx as a Data & Tech Hub for the commercial vehicle sector

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Commercial Vehicles

## At a glance

Founding of tb.lx, Daimler Truck's deep-tech hub — strategy, positioning, HR and scale to the first 30 employees, turning trucks into connected ecosystems.

## Initial situation

Daimler recognized the need to evolve beyond producing trucks and buses into offering digitally connected, intelligent and sustainable transportation solutions, but lacked dedicated teams capable of building and scaling platforms for connected-vehicle ecosystems.

## Approach

KI group founded the initial company idea, strategy and positioning of tb.lx — designed to operate with startup agility while leveraging Daimler's industrial strength. We supported internal marketing and alignment, implemented an HR and recruiting strategy and scaled to the first 30 employees.

## Results

- **Data connectivity** implemented into the commercial vehicle sector — transforming trucks into connected ecosystems
- Corporate startup enabled **faster product cycles**, experimentation and cross-functional collaboration
- **Unlocked mobility patterns** through big data, creating innovative digital products
- **Sustainable transportation** through technology
