# Tamanna — Data Governance Concept Development

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Retail, E-Commerce & Omnichannel

## At a glance

A role-based data governance concept with quality measures and a data catalogue, ensuring secure, accurate and discoverable data across regions.

## Initial situation

Multiple teams (Engineering, Marketing, Customer Success, Management) accessed the data platform without a shared understanding of data needs, access, quality or accuracy. There was no framework for data validation or consistent timestamp accuracy across regions.

## Approach

We conceptualized a governance framework spanning data types (raw, cleansed, curated) and categories to define role-based access. Data quality measures were introduced to maintain validity and accuracy across the organization.

## Results

- **Secure, reliable, valid data access** from day one of platform development
- Transactional **data accuracy and usability** increased for finance and reporting
- **Data catalogue** created with glossary to enable discovery across the organization
