# BASF — Largest CoPilot Integration in Europe

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Chemical

## At a glance

One of Europe's largest M365 Copilot rollouts — a one-year enablement program scaling AI adoption to 36,000 BASF employees across four waves.

## Initial situation

BASF, a global chemical leader, launched one of Europe's largest Microsoft 365 Copilot rollouts, equipping 36,000 employees with AI-powered tools to boost efficiency and enable smarter collaboration.

## Approach

In collaboration with Microsoft we ran a one-year enablement program combining strategic planning with modular activation. Four rollout waves, 49 change agents and a real-time dashboard drove use case discovery, scalable onboarding and long-term adoption through curated content, expert support and the gamified 'Copilot Flight Mission'.

## Results

- Supported **M365 Copilot adoption for 36k employees** internationally
- Defined **communication strategy** to align all stakeholders
- **Sustained training** efforts across the organization
- Managed a **user community** to foster peer-to-peer learning
