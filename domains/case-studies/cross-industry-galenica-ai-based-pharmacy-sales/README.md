# Galenica — AI-Based Pharmacy Sales

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Retail, E-Commerce & Omnichannel

## At a glance

A Gen AI sales assistant trained on Galenica's full product documentation, giving pharmacists instant, targeted support and cutting support team costs.

## Initial situation

Staff across Galenica's 400 pharmacies couldn't effectively conduct sales, verify prescriptions or confirm prices due to franchise-wide sales tool problems, while sales and IT support teams struggled to troubleshoot tool queries.

## Approach

We created a fully customized Gen AI sales assistant, trained on Galenica's entire product documentation, now offering targeted responses to both pharmacists and Galenica's sales and IT support teams.

## Results

- For pharmacists: **instant access** to services, customized support and **faster processing times**
- For Galenica: **satisfaction increase** through targeted service and support
- **Reduced support team costs** due to lower demand
