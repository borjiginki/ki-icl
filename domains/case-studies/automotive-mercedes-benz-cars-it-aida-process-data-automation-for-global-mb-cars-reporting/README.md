# Mercedes-Benz Cars IT — AIDA — Process & Data Automation for global MB Cars Reporting

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · IT

## At a glance

Global automated sales-data reporting (AIDA) for Mercedes-Benz Cars — vehicle-level transparency replacing manual Excel consolidation across worldwide markets.

## Initial situation

Monthly sales data from worldwide markets was manually generated by consolidating sales and stock data across departments and entities — inefficient and error-prone, relying on market systems or manual Excel uploads into the central market and product information system.

## Approach

KI group presented a concept focused on process automation for sales data, initiating modeling, optimization and standardization of worldwide sales and reporting processes with hybrid project management, and taking responsibility for the full realization and global rollout of an automated sales-data reporting system on vehicle level.

## Results

- **Optimization** of daily sales and stock control across markets through increased transparency and vehicle-level data
- Significant increase of **efficiency and data quality** via automation of formerly manual processes
- Strong **stakeholder buy-in** by showcasing positive impact even without additional resources
