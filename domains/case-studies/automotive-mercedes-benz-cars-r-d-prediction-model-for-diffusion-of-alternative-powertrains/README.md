# Mercedes-Benz Cars R&D — Prediction model for diffusion of alternative powertrains

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · R&D

## At a glance

An analytical model on 1M+ car buyers predicting EV/PHEV adoption — regional cluster models and a Power BI front end for Mercedes-Benz R&D.

## Initial situation

Ahead of launching a wave of new electrified products (EVs and plug-in hybrids), Mercedes-Benz needed to understand which type of customer — and how many — would be prepared to switch to alternative powertrains.

## Approach

We developed an analytical model fusing statistical algorithms with deep market and business understanding, mass-analyzing existing data and turning it into a reusable, cost-efficient data product.

## Results

- Data from **+1 million new car buyers** transformed into a data-driven behavioral analysis
- **Standardized regional cluster models** for Europe, USA and China with nine distinct buyer types each
- Provision of a **Power BI front end** to deep-dive into customer behavior and brand development
