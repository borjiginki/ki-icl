# Sartorius — Visual Anomaly Detection

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Pharma & Life Sciences

## At a glance

An AI vision system for Sartorius that classifies and measures production anomalies in medical film, ensuring quality, compliance and efficient defect handling.

## Initial situation

In medical film production, anomalies can jeopardize product quality, create regulatory challenges and affect customer satisfaction. An automated, precise solution for detecting and analyzing defects was needed to ensure the highest quality standards.

## Approach

We implemented an AI-supported solution that classifies and analyzes anomalies during production. After classification, anomaly size is calculated using an image segmentation model, and the system informs the production team if the product is impaired.

## Results

- **Improved product quality and compliance** through precise anomaly detection
- **Increased customer satisfaction** through consistent quality control
- **More efficient production** via automated defect detection and notification
