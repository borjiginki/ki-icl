# Mercedes-Benz — Churn Prediction

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Data & Analytics

## At a glance

An AI churn-prediction MVP for Mercedes-Benz — consolidating customer data into a model and dashboard that surfaces churn risk for data-driven retention.

## Initial situation

Mercedes-Benz collects extensive customer data with consent, offering the potential to predict future customer behavior — an opportunity that had largely remained untapped.

## Approach

Available data sources were consolidated and analyzed for potential use cases. Together with the client, a churn prediction model was selected as the first MVP and implemented, retrospectively identifying customer churn and providing insight into future behavioral patterns.

## Results

- Development of a **'Churn Hypothesis'** with the client
- **Consolidation and analysis** of numerous data sources
- Creation of an **AI model for churn-rate prediction**
- **Intuitive dashboard** supporting data-driven retention decisions
