# Ströer — Out of Home Advertising AI

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Media & Advertising

## At a glance

An AI that checks posters against 10 content criteria and validates dimensions, dramatically speeding up Ströer's ad approval while improving quality.

## Initial situation

Reviewing and approving customer advertisements — especially posters — was complex and time-intensive. Both Ströer and clients invested significant effort to verify ads met content guidelines and fit the physical dimensions of advertising spaces, causing delays and inefficiencies.

## Approach

We introduced an AI solution working in two steps: first it analyzes posters against 10 content criteria (readability, color contrast, branding); second it verifies exact dimensions for proper fit in the designated spaces — streamlining the approval workflow while maintaining quality and consistency.

## Results

- Significantly **faster approval** through AI-driven automation — saving time and resources
- **Reduction of manual errors**, improving ad quality and customer experience
- **Increased capacity** to handle larger ad volumes simultaneously
