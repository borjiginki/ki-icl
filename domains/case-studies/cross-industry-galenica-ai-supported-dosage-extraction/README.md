# Galenica — AI-Supported Dosage Extraction

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Retail, E-Commerce & Omnichannel

## At a glance

A GenAI solution that converts unstructured medical dosage texts into structured, queryable data with confidence scoring for fast expert validation.

## Initial situation

Galenica's daughter company HCI Solutions maintains an index of all medical products in Switzerland, including dosages. These dosages exist only as unstructured free text from manufacturers and cannot be queried or filtered.

## Approach

We built a GenAI solution that extracts dosages into a structured format from the manufacturers' free texts. After expert validation, this structured format supports multiple use cases, including a user-friendly search interface.

## Results

- **Fully automated extraction** of complex dosage structures
- **Confidence indications** enabling efficient validation by medical experts
- Basis for a **wide range of additional use cases**
