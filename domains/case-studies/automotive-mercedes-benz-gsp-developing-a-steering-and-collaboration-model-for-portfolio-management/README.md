# Mercedes-Benz GSP — Developing a steering and collaboration model for portfolio management

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · IT

## At a glance

A Jira-based portfolio management and steering model for Mercedes-Benz After Sales — OKRs, governance boards and a roadmap to remove request bottlenecks.

## Initial situation

After Sales digital products were organizationally transferred from GSP/BW to MxD/MC, but a structured process for prioritizing and processing new product requests was missing — leading to unmanaged requests, lack of focus and resource bottlenecks.

## Approach

Jointly with the client we designed a target steering model through OKRs and Guiding Principles, then developed a Jira-based portfolio management process backed by governance structures (committees, escalation instances, control mechanisms) and documented the approach holistically on Confluence.

## Results

- **Cohesive portfolio process** for seamless collaboration and capacity planning
- **OKR-based steering model** supported by Guiding Principles and portfolio priorities
- Establishment of **Boards, Panels and Roles** to support the process
- Step-by-step **implementation roadmap**
