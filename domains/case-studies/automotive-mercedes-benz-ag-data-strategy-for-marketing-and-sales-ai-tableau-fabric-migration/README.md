# Mercedes-Benz AG — Data strategy for marketing and sales (AI Tableau→Fabric migration)

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Data & Analytics

## At a glance

An AI multi-agent solution migrating Mercedes-Benz from Tableau to Microsoft Fabric/Power BI — up to 80% faster, 70% lower cost, with centralized governance.

## Initial situation

The client relied heavily on Tableau for reporting but faced high costs, limited scalability and fragmented governance. Manual migration to Microsoft Fabric and Power BI was complex, time-consuming and error-prone; a faster, automated, future-proof migration approach was needed.

## Approach

We developed an AI-powered multi-agent solution to automate every stage of the migration — analyzing the Tableau environment, converting calculations into optimized DAX, transforming ETL logic, precisely recreating native visuals, auto-generating semantic models and centralizing governance in a global migration cockpit.

## Results

- **Accelerated migration by up to 80%** with minimal manual effort
- **Reduced reporting and migration costs by up to 70%**
- Delivered **accurate, ready-to-use Power BI reports**
- Enhanced **governance and transparency** across the BI environment
- Enabled advanced **AI-driven analytics** for future scalability
