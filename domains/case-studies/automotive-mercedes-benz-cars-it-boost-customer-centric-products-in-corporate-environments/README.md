# Mercedes-Benz Cars IT — Boost customer-centric products in corporate environments

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · IT

## At a glance

Product strategy for a 100k-employee HR web app at Mercedes-Benz — clear positioning, a department-integration roadmap and a value-driven implementation framework.

## Initial situation

The client used a web app as a central touchpoint for around 100k employees to record and view personal data (working hours, absences, salary). The app lacked strategic and technological embedding in IT and integration with specialist departments.

## Approach

We defined the product's Purpose, Vision and Mission, a roadmap for integrating specialist departments and a framework for product development.

## Results

- **Clear positioning of the HR product** and cross-department alignment (strategy, specialist departments, product, IT)
- **Time and cost savings** through digitalization and automation
- **Value-proposition-oriented implementation framework** for business and IT
