# Siemens — AI-driven Solution & Enterprise Architecture

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Industry

## At a glance

Assisted, AI-supported enterprise architecture design that aligns stakeholders, detects issues early and protects architectural integrity at scale.

## Initial situation

Siemens faced challenges in architectural design: knowledge was hard to access, many stakeholders needed alignment (causing delays and quality inconsistencies), code drifted from designed architectures, and architects lacked capacity to support growth at scale.

## Approach

We introduced assisted architecture design to improve alignment, compliance and usability — closing the gap between planning and execution. Automated validation with embedded enterprise knowledge enabled early issue detection, while continuous checks safeguarded architectural integrity.

## Results

- Improved **knowledge access** and **decision making**
- **Increased output** of high-value but low-capacity teams
- **Streamlined architecture validation** with embedded AI agents
