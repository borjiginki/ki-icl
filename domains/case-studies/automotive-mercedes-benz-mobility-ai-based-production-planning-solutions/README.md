# Mercedes-Benz Mobility — AI-based Production Planning Solutions

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Production

## At a glance

A multi-agent AI concept for Mercedes-Benz Mobility production planning — faster, more accurate precedence-sequence generation for new car lines from historical data.

## Initial situation

Production planning at MB Mobility relies heavily on static, rule-based systems and the experience of process planners, requiring alignment across stakeholders for parts lists, work instructions (AVO) and a precedence sequence for line balancing. Creating these sequences for a new car line is time-consuming and labor-intensive.

## Approach

Our multidisciplinary team explored the context and solution space and proposed two solutions, the first a multi-agent system — starting with one agent — to support precedence-sequence generation, and derived three different precedence sequences based on historical data.

## Results

- **Faster initial generation** of precedence sequences across production halls
- **More accurate** precedence sequences by leveraging historical ones
- Development of a **multi-agent system concept** to reduce alignment time and improve digital data reliability
