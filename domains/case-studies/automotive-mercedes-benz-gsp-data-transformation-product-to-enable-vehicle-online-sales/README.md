# Mercedes-Benz GSP — Data transformation product to enable vehicle online sales

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Sales

## At a glance

A FOSS-based data transformation product enabling Mercedes-Benz online vehicle sales — validating and transforming upstream data with a quality-monitoring dashboard.

## Initial situation

Mercedes-Benz made the strategic decision to sell vehicles online for the first time, bypassing the established retail sales channel — an experiment that quickly became a strategic priority alongside the broader change in the sales model.

## Approach

We selected and combined FOSS elements in a unique configuration to collect data from numerous upstream systems, check, validate and transform it, and hand completed sets to the online store to make cars sellable — plus a dashboard to monitor data completeness and transformation success.

## Results

- **Stable productive environment** for a crucial business process
- **4-dimensional scaling** (markets, models, vehicles, users and their individual rule sets)
- **Reduced production time** per transformed unit and resource capacity usage
