# Mercedes-Benz Cars Marketing & Sales — Enablement of Marketing and Sales Department

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Enablement

## At a glance

A large-scale BI and Power BI enablement for Mercedes-Benz Cars Marketing & Sales — tailor-made training delivered to ~600 people across multiple waves.

## Initial situation

The Marketing & Sales organization in the Mercedes-Benz Cars division was the focus of an extensive digitalization offensive, with targeted measures in automation and business intelligence such as Power BI.

## Approach

KI performance developed tailor-made training formats for the marketing and sales sector with various requirements; in several waves a total of around 600 people were trained.

## Results

- **Extraordinary demand** and positive feedback for the trainings
- A total of about **600 participants** successfully trained
- Both **on-site and virtual** trainings provided depending on conditions
