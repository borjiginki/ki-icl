# Mercedes-Benz Mobility — Transformation of Finance & Controlling into a product-driven department

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Financial Services

## At a glance

A BI and product-driven transformation of MB Mobility's Finance & Controlling — purpose/vision/mission, a roadmap and a new operating model across IT and markets.

## Initial situation

The Finance & Controlling department of Mercedes-Benz Mobility wanted to go beyond necessary technical modernization and also change its working model, asking us to set up a comprehensive BI transformation program and implement a product-driven working model.

## Approach

We developed Purpose, Vision and Mission and derived a transformation roadmap combining tech-stack modernization with the shift to a product-driven model across IT, global business units and local markets, guiding teams through the initial phase with new ceremonies and workflows.

## Results

- Strategic direction set with **Purpose, Vision, Mission**
- Clear rules via defined **Guiding Principles** and adjusted roles and responsibilities
- Overview of potential digital products for a **lean portfolio**
- Implemented **ceremonies and workflows** to operationalize the roadmap
