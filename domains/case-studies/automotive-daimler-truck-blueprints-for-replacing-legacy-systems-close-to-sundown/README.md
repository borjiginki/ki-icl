# Daimler Truck — Blueprints for replacing legacy systems close to sundown

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · IT

## At a glance

A legacy-replacement blueprint for Daimler Truck's legal office — complexity metrics, platform selection and migration scenarios for a time-critical sundown.

## Initial situation

Daimler Truck was about to ramp down the core system for its legal office, as it was not supported by the target infrastructure. A new platform, reduced dependency on third-party providers and a time-critical migration were required.

## Approach

We supported the client by selecting one of eight platform modules, created a set of metrics to estimate the legacy system's complexity and defined migration target scenarios.

## Results

- Delivery of a **blueprint for evaluating complex software systems**
- Selection of a **new platform** for the Legal Office systems
- **Clear roadmap** for further actions
