# Mercedes-Benz Leasing Deutschland — Design of product organization and introduction of steering model

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Financial Services

## At a glance

A product-driven organizational redesign for MB Leasing Deutschland — vision, target picture, OKRs and Team Topologies to empower its dev and platform teams.

## Initial situation

Mercedes-Benz Leasing Deutschland (MBLD) aimed to build the digital leasing products of the future in an E2E journey from vehicle customization to contract renewal. Its four dev and six platform teams worked in a software-development mode with rigid practices and limited empowerment.

## Approach

We guided MBLD to design their organizational target picture and transform into a product-driven company with customer-centric digital products, introducing Team Topologies, OKRs and Guiding Principles to steer and facilitate the working model.

## Results

- Definition of **Vision, Mission and Organizational Target Picture** as a North Star
- Implementation of **Guiding Principles, OKRs and Team Topologies** to foster independence and performance
- Support of **SAFe elements** adapted to MBLD's context
