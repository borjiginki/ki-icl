# Mercedes-Benz Bank — Optimization of trade cycle management for a captive bank

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Financial Services

## At a glance

A data-driven, predictive trade-cycle-management blueprint for Mercedes-Benz Bank — stage-by-stage OKRs and a new working model to lift customer retention.

## Initial situation

Customer retention of MB finance customers was significantly below the mid-term strategic target. Enhanced trade cycle management (TCM) was identified as a means to preventively steer customer behavior ahead of the natural run-out of lease and finance contracts.

## Approach

We deeply analyzed the existing process and its shortcomings, dissected it into increments, identified improvement opportunities and analytics objectives per stage, and recombined them into a holistic, redesigned process.

## Results

- Blueprint for a **data-driven, predictive Trade Cycle Management** at every process stage
- **OKRs** determined for every stage of the process
- Proposed a **flexible, individual and integrated working model** to reorganize the TCM process
