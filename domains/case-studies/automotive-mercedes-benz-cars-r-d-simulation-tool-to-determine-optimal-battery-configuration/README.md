# Mercedes-Benz Cars R&D — Simulation tool to determine optimal battery configuration

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · R&D

## At a glance

A machine-learning simulation of real-life driving profiles for Mercedes-Benz, predicting EV range requirements per segment with a 1–3% margin of error.

## Initial situation

To optimize EV market acceptance, battery capacity must meet the real-life requirements of a defined percentage of customers — requiring deep insight into actual driving behavior, which was only available in fragments.

## Approach

We built a machine learning model from multiple partial-insight data sources to simulate large-scale, real-life-identical driving profiles.

## Results

- ML model put to practical use to **predict real-life market requirements** by customer group, market and model type
- **Margin of error** for key predictions (EV range suitable for X% of customers per segment) as low as **1–3%**
