# Degussa Bank — AI-Tool for Automatic Document Recognition

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Finance

## At a glance

An AI tool that scans Degussa Bank's document archive to identify contract-relevant files automatically — cutting overall expenditure by a factor of six.

## Initial situation

For a bank with 40+ years of history and over a million customers, managing an extensive inventory of scanned documents is a major challenge — manual identification is time-consuming, costly and error-prone.

## Approach

We developed an advanced AI tool that automatically analyzes the bank's electronic archive to identify and filter specific, contract-relevant documents, streamlining the process and significantly reducing manual checks.

## Results

- Cost reduction that **cut overall expenditure by a factor of six**
- **Efficiency gains** enabling more effective document management and strategic resource deployment
