# ADAC — Interactive Customer Database

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Services

## At a glance

An AI-driven interactive app for ADAC — a centralized knowledge engine delivering 24/7 personalized support, boosting engagement, acquisition and loyalty.

## Initial situation

ADAC's members, employees and partners faced growing complexity navigating a wide range of services. Members increasingly expect instant, personalized, easy-to-use digital support, so ADAC aimed to improve access to information, services and offers across all channels and times.

## Approach

We developed an interactive app with personalized planning tools, tailored recommendations and advanced search. Knowledge is consolidated into a centralized, AI-driven engine delivering 24/7 personalized support and seamless access to all services.

## Results

- Increased **visibility and accessibility** of the service portfolio
- Stronger **member engagement** through personalized experiences
- Improved **acquisition** of new members
- Enhanced **loyalty** through tailored cross- and up-selling
- **Improved service quality** and overall satisfaction
