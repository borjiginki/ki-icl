# Mercedes-Benz Vans — Review and reconstruction of Van Online Configurator

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Sales

## At a glance

A holistic review of the Mercedes-Benz Van Online Configurator — 25+ improvement areas and a restructuring proposal validated by market research to grow online sales.

## Initial situation

The MB Van strategy targets a significantly higher share of online-generated sales. The Van Online Configurator (VOC), a crucial element of the digital sales funnel, lacked certain functionalities and was suspected of sub-standard performance and user experience.

## Approach

We performed a holistic review of the current VOC — technical performance and competitive comparison, qualitative content benchmarking, UI/UX review and the business value of additional features — and used the findings to create a roadmap toward a cutting-edge VOC.

## Results

- Identified **>25 major points of improvement** across architecture, performance, usability and business quality
- Created a **concrete visual proposal** for restructuring the VOC
- Hypotheses and proposals **tested and confirmed** in subsequent market research
