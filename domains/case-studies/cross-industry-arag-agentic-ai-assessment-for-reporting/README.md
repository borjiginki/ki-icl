# ARAG — Agentic AI Assessment for Reporting

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Insurance

## At a glance

A multi-agent AI architecture for ARAG's SFCR reporting that validates, cross-checks and drafts — cutting manual GRM workload while improving audit quality.

## Initial situation

ARAG needed to automate parts of the SFCR report generation process — a BaFin regulatory requirement on solvency and financial condition. The existing process was highly manual, involving multiple departments and significant effort from the Group Risk Management (GRM) team.

## Approach

We conducted a deep-dive assessment and designed a multi-agent AI architecture in which multiple agents collaborate to validate departmental inputs, cross-check data and generate a high-quality draft, with a final agent consolidating results — reducing manual workload and improving accuracy.

## Results

- **Cost reduction** through process automation
- **Improved report quality** with AI-powered validation
- **Future-proof setup** aligned with BaFin's increasing AI adoption
- **Higher efficiency** and reduced GRM workload
- **Easier audit affirmation** through consistent, traceable reporting
