# Mercedes-Benz Vans — Gen AI Use Case enablement and prompt engineering

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Enablement

## At a glance

Interactive Gen AI and prompt-engineering workshops for Mercedes-Benz Vans — hands-on enablement that seeded company-wide adoption of AI-assisted processes.

## Initial situation

Mercedes-Benz Vans identified the need to build foundational and advanced competencies in Generative AI, particularly the effective use of Large Language Models, enabling departments to understand, assess and leverage AI-driven tools and processes.

## Approach

We designed an interactive workshop format on Gen AI use case enablement and prompt engineering, combining theory with practical exercises and encouraging participants to develop their own use cases through real-life examples and collaborative problem-solving.

## Results

- Participants gained **conceptual knowledge and hands-on skills** in Generative AI and prompt engineering
- Laid the groundwork for **company-wide adoption** of AI-assisted processes
- Fostered a culture of **continuous learning and innovation**
