# Siemens — AI-driven UI/UX Quality Control

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Industry

## At a glance

A scalable AI solution that automates UI/UX quality checks across Siemens applications, ensuring consistent standards and unlocking new revenue streams.

## Initial situation

Controlling UI/UX quality across Siemens' internal and external applications relied on time-consuming, inefficient manual processes. Standards were inconsistently applied, knowledge was fragmented, design impact lacked visibility and scalability was limited.

## Approach

Our solution enabled consistent, scalable UI/UX quality checks following shared design standards and best practices. We automated value delivery, unlocked new business opportunities and ensured scalable impact, with AI capabilities continuously improved and tailored to needs.
