# Mercedes-Benz Cloudmobility — Special-use cloud platform for mobility and transportation

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Mobility

## At a glance

cloudmobility — a compliant, special-purpose automotive cloud built by KI group (incl. CTO/CPO and 30 engineers), connecting devices, services and infrastructure at scale.

## Initial situation

The mobility sector needed seamless, data-driven services while reducing costs and increasing flexibility. Existing solutions were fragmented and lacked scalability, creating the need for a compliant, independent automotive cloud to connect devices, services and infrastructure.

## Approach

We built a specialized automotive cloud platform combining infrastructure, data and service layers in a single ecosystem, with European compliance and data control embedded by design. A KI group core team including the CTO, CPO and 30 engineers ensured strategic leadership and rapid, high-quality delivery.

## Results

- Delivered a **holistic platform** for scalable, connected mobility services
- Enabled new **digital business models** with independent infrastructure
- Ensured **strict compliance** and data sovereignty
- Achieved **rapid execution** and high-quality delivery
- **Positioned the client as a leader** in future mobility innovation
