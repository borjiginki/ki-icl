# Mercedes-Benz VDS — Centralizing Vehicle Data for E-Commerce

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Sales

## At a glance

The Vehicle Data Store for Mercedes-Benz — a scalable backend/frontend ensuring only e-commerce-ready vehicles go live, with agile delivery and modern DevOps.

## Initial situation

Mercedes-Benz required a central solution to collect, transform and enrich vehicle data from downstream systems so only e-commerce-ready vehicles appear in online stores across Europe. The fragmented data landscape made it hard to maintain consistency, transparency and control over vehicle data quality and lifecycle.

## Approach

We designed and operated a scalable backend and frontend for the Vehicle Data Store, acting as Product Owner alongside Mercedes-Benz, focusing on agile development, roadmap planning, workflow optimization and continuous delivery with modern DevOps.

## Results

- **Increased transparency and control** of vehicle data across markets
- **Improved data quality** and lifecycle management
- **Faster, more structured development** through agile collaboration
- **Scalable DevOps setup** enabling continuous product growth
