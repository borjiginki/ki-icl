# Galenica — AI-Driven Order Recommendations

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Retail, E-Commerce & Omnichannel

## At a glance

An AI ordering solution for Galenica's 400 pharmacies that factors in seasonality and shelf-life, boosting turnover and cutting waste from expired stock.

## Initial situation

Galenica's 400 pharmacies each manage their own stock but use a central system to order new products. The system ignored seasonal and shelf-life factors, forcing pharmacists to manually adjust quantities — leading to waste and inefficiency.

## Approach

We created a solution that categorizes and recommends inventory accounting for seasonal demand and shelf-life, providing optimal just-in-time order suggestions based on historical data analysis and AI for calculating amounts.

## Results

- **Increased pharmacy turnover** thanks to demand-oriented stock
- **Optimized storage costs**
- Significantly **less expired medicine** and minimized losses
