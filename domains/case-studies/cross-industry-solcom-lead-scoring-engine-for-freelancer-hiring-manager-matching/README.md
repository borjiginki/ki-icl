# Solcom — Lead Scoring Engine for Freelancer–Hiring Manager Matching

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** IT & Engineering Freelance Staffing

## At a glance

An AI-powered lead generation pipeline that classifies roles and matches hiring managers, opening millions of DACH companies as scalable touchpoints.

## Initial situation

Solcom connects companies with freelance tech and engineering talent. While sourcing freelancers is relatively easy, acquiring new client leads and identifying the right hiring managers was a highly repetitive, manual and time-intensive challenge.

## Approach

We built an automated pipeline to identify high-interest jobs and connect them with suitable hiring managers. A Python script gathers and cleans data, while an AI agent classifies roles, identifies hiring managers via LinkedIn profiles and matches job postings to those managers — running end-to-end from a manual trigger.

## Results

- **Reduced manual effort** with greater accuracy and much **faster lead generation**
- **Millions of companies** in the DACH region now reachable as touchpoints — impossible to achieve manually
