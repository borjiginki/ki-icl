# Telekom — Chat with your Finance Data

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Telecommunication

## At a glance

A Microsoft Fabric report with AI functions and Data Agents enabling natural-language finance interaction, automated commentary and consistent narratives.

## Initial situation

The finance department faced manual, fragmented commenting processes across reports, slowing financial reporting and limiting collaboration between business units and HQ. Insights were scattered across tools, causing inconsistent narratives and high coordination effort.

## Approach

We implemented a report in Microsoft Fabric enhanced with AI functions, Data Agents and analytical taskflows. The solution enables natural-language interaction with finance data, automates commentary and standardizes financial narratives across stakeholders on a scalable, governed Fabric architecture.

## Results

- **Faster, more efficient** financial reporting cycles
- **Consistent, AI-supported** financial narratives
- Improved **collaboration** between business units and HQ
- **Scalable, high-performance Fabric** foundation for future AI finance cases
