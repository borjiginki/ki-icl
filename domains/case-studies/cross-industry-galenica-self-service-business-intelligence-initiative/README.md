# Galenica — Self-Service Business Intelligence Initiative

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Retail, E-Commerce & Omnichannel

## At a glance

A self-service BI strategy on MS Fabric for Galenica — harmonized KPIs, a top-down blueprint and a bottom-up solution that grows with new stakeholders.

## Initial situation

Galenica departments (Finance, Accounting, Control, Inventory Management) used legacy Oracle software for their data needs. Key measures and KPI definitions were not harmonized internally, and the legacy tool offered poor reporting and data discovery.

## Approach

We followed a grass-roots approach involving different stakeholder groups to harmonize KPI definitions and realign the reporting architecture. We developed a detailed SSBI strategy focused on business value and utility per stakeholder persona, with technical infrastructure based on MS Fabric.

## Results

- **Blueprint** for the Self-Service infrastructure (top-down approach)
- Identification of **white-spots** in inter-departmental KPI understanding
- **Self-Service BI solution** in MS Fabric (bottom-up), organically extensible
