# Galenica — NPS Categorization & Analysis

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Retail, E-Commerce & Omnichannel

## At a glance

A Generative AI solution that categorizes free-text NPS comments for Galenica, turning unstructured feedback into quick, actionable management insights.

## Initial situation

Like most companies, Galenica tracks its Net Promoter Score via feedback forms and third-party surveys. While numeric scores are easy to assess, free-text comments are hard to evaluate.

## Approach

We developed a Generative AI solution that categorizes respondents' free-text comments, enabling efficient grouping and sorting of feedback to highlight key improvement areas and uncover potential market opportunities.

## Results

- **Fully automated categorization** of free-text NPS comments
- **Grouping and sorting** of feedback
- **Quick insights** for management
