# Telekom — Retail Operations Cockpit

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Telecommunication

## At a glance

A centralized Retail Operations Cockpit connecting 650+ shops with HQ, enabling faster issue resolution and better data-driven offerings.

## Initial situation

Telekom Privatkunden Vertriebsgesellschaft (PVG) manages over 650 Telekom shops and partner agencies as well as approx. 1,500 integrated shops in the retail sector. The aim was to improve communication and collaboration with the shops at headquarters and to create a centralized system for sharing data and best practices.

## Approach

We delivered a centralized system — the Retail Operations Cockpit — for communication across organizational silos in sales and customer service for private customers. Our user-centric approach helped employees resolve issues faster and create better offerings through effective data availability.

## Results

- **Productivity gains** and **higher employee satisfaction** achieved
- Increased number of tickets resolved seamlessly
- **EBIT increase** through capabilities developed to provide the optimal offer
