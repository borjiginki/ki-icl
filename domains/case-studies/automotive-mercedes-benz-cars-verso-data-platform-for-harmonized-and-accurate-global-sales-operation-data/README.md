# Mercedes-Benz Cars (Verso) — Data platform for harmonized and accurate global sales operation data

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Data & Analytics

## At a glance

A consolidated global sales-operations data platform for Mercedes-Benz (Verso) — self-service access and tailored dashboards for consistent, transparent insight.

## Initial situation

The client struggled to harmonize sales operations data across units. Fragmented systems led to inefficiencies, unreliable data for decision-making and an inability to provide comprehensive insights at different levels of granularity.

## Approach

We created a data platform consolidating sales data and empowering the organization with data-driven insights. A self-service solution made comprehensive, reliable data easily accessible, with customized dashboards for executive, management and operational needs.

## Results

- **Improved data consistency** across all reports and dashboards and increased transparency
- **Enhanced decision-making** through actionable insights
- **Unlocked growth potential** via an overview of global sales operations data
- **Streamlined operations** through department-tailored reports
