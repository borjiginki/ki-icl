# Mercedes-Benz Cars IT — Product cut and steering of a Software Product Portfolio

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · IT

## At a glance

Portfolio and steering redesign for 53 Mercedes-Benz software teams — OKRs, guiding principles and product canvases for a lean, transparent product portfolio.

## Initial situation

During the transformation from on-premise software to a cloud environment, the IT department supporting Mercedes-Benz car production wanted an agile, customer-focused approach and a more transparent product portfolio — redefining and reducing existing products and introducing new ones.

## Approach

We focused on two areas — Steering Models and Product Portfolio Management. We aligned Guiding Principles, defined core-team roles and set up OKRs, and enabled product teams to formulate Product Canvases and define dependencies among products.

## Results

- Steering a large organization of **53 software development teams** via OKRs, Guiding Principles and new roles
- **Reduction of outdated and redundant** software products and limited dependencies
- **Increased transparency** of product content, ownership and value within a Lean Product Portfolio
