# Chemondis by Lanxess — Bootstrapping Europe's largest chemical marketplace

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Chemical Industry

## At a glance

Built Europe's largest chemical marketplace from scratch — fully operational in six months with ~50 FTE and a near nine-figure monthly transaction volume.

## Initial situation

The chemical industry is complex and built on trust. To streamline distribution channels and increase sales, Lanxess needed a solution to offer their products on a single, trusted marketplace — covering all companies and all products.

## Approach

Together with Lanxess we built a chemical marketplace from scratch. KI performance developed the business plan, engineered the platform, hired the operating team and executed the go-to-market. Within six months the marketplace was fully operational, employing ~50 full-time staff and nearing a nine-figure monthly transaction volume.

## Results

- **Consolidation of sales activities** in one marketplace
- **Reduction of manual efforts and errors**, freeing up resources
- **Higher sales** driven by enhanced transparency and usability for the buyer's side
