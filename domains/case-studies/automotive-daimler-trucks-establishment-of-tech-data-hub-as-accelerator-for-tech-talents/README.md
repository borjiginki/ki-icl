# Daimler Trucks — Establishment of Tech & Data Hub as accelerator for tech talents

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Commercial Vehicles

## At a glance

A Lisbon Data & Tech Hub built ground-up for Daimler Trucks — vision, governance, legal setup, first teams and global partnerships accelerating innovation.

## Initial situation

Daimler Trucks aimed to strengthen its position as a technology leader by creating a Data & Tech Hub to drive innovation, execution and scaling of data-driven solutions — building the hub from the ground up, including strategy, governance, talent and ecosystem integration.

## Approach

We conceived and executed the Lisbon-based hub, providing strategic leadership and delivery capacity — defining vision, governance and operating model, setting up the legal and organizational structure, recruiting the first engineering and product teams and building global ecosystem partnerships.

## Results

- Established a **fully operational, scalable hub** in Lisbon
- Built and onboarded a **high-performing tech and product team**
- Launched **initial platform projects** delivering measurable business value
- Integrated the hub into a **global innovation ecosystem**
- **Accelerated innovation and execution speed** across Daimler Trucks
