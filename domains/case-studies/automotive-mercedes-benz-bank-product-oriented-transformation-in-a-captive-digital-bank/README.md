# Mercedes-Benz Bank — Product-oriented Transformation in a captive Digital Bank

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Financial Services

## At a glance

An adapted SAFe transformation for Mercedes-Benz Bank — OKRs, guiding principles and product roles across 400+ people in 13 streams, targeting >50% OPEX reduction by 2027.

## Initial situation

Mercedes-Benz Bank faced critical operational and competitive disadvantages due to inefficient processes and an oversized organization. A lean, modern and nimble set-up was needed to reduce OPEX by >50% until 2027 for a portfolio worth billions and to gain the leading market position in the automotive financial sector.

## Approach

We introduced an adapted SAFe framework, leveraging product-oriented elements such as OKRs and Guiding Principles to drive the transformation across the entire organization — more than 400 people in 13 streams.

## Results

- Adapted **SAFe and further agile methodologies** introduced on large scale
- **OKRs on program level** implemented
- **Product-related roles** defined and enabled
- **Target Operating Model** conceptualized
- Organizational **foundation for transformation** set
