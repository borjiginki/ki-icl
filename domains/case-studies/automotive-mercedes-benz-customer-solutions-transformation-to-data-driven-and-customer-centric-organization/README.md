# Mercedes-Benz Customer Solutions — Transformation to data-driven and customer-centric organization

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Data & Governance

## At a glance

A data-driven, customer-centric transformation for MB Customer Solutions — data management, a new architecture and audit-ready governance derived from a global policy.

## Initial situation

The client initiated a transformation of organization, governance and architecture to become more data-driven and customer-centric, starting with the introduction of a global 80-page Data Governance Policy.

## Approach

We supported implementation of data management and a new data architecture, deriving governance requirements from global MB policies, ensuring audit-readiness by aligning technical and organizational setup with policy standards and establishing a working model from idea to execution.

## Results

- Defined **data management guidelines** and practices
- New **data architecture** introduced
- New **governance and working model** implemented
