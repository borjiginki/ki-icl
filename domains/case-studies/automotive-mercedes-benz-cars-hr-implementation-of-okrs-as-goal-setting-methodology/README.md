# Mercedes-Benz Cars HR — Implementation of OKRs as goal-setting methodology

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · HR

## At a glance

An OKR goal-setting rollout for Mercedes-Benz Cars HR — department/team OKRs, a knowledge hub and steering model that accelerated team integration and change.

## Initial situation

Due to an organizational change within the HR department, the client wanted to implement OKRs as a goal-setting methodology to align and steer teams with diverse requirements and motivate them to work in a self-determined, efficient way.

## Approach

We defined OKRs at department and team level, implemented them through various sessions and a knowledge hub on the client's intranet, and tracked progress across the newly established teams.

## Results

- **OKRs on department and team level** defined
- Enabled the client to work with **OKRs as an efficient steering model**
- **Knowledge hub** for teams and team leads developed
- **Accelerated integration** of new colleagues and successful change in team setup
