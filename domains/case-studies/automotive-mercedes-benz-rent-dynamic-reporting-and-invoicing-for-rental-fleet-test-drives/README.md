# Mercedes-Benz Rent — Dynamic Reporting and Invoicing for Rental Fleet & Test Drives

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Sales

## At a glance

A Power BI reporting and invoicing platform for MB Rent's vehicle booking system — full transparency on fleet utilization and automated dealer settlements.

## Initial situation

Mercedes-Benz fleet managers lacked a proper reporting system for their Vehicle Booking System (VBS), unable to track vehicle movements or oversee transactions — preventing automated settlements between MB and retailers.

## Approach

We assessed business processes and tailored data modeling to optimize performance. With a robust data pipeline ensuring reliable information, the migration to Microsoft Power BI enabled a seamless transition of existing KPIs and MB Rent reports.

## Results

- **Improved accessibility** to vehicle utilization data of the MB rental fleet
- **Increased operational efficiency** with a dynamic, performant reporting platform
- Frontend for **automated settlement** with retailers
