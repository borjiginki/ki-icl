# MC Sonae — Supercharging SDLC with AI

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Retail & Omnichannel

## At a glance

An AI embedded in Jira that surfaces relevant code, docs and tickets and auto-generates unit tests — speeding onboarding, delivery and test coverage.

## Initial situation

Development teams faced major inefficiencies due to fragmented knowledge across Jira, repos and Confluence — causing long investigation cycles and heavy reliance on senior engineers. Low test coverage and poor documentation further blocked scalability and reduced development speed.

## Approach

We embedded an AI into Jira to surface relevant code, docs and past tickets instantly. Combined with an agentic system for auto-generating unit tests, this improved onboarding speed, test coverage and overall development velocity and quality.

## Results

- **Faster ticket resolution** — teams reported less time hunting for context
- Engineering execution became **faster and more productive**
- Made **critical knowledge accessible** to every engineer
