# Daimler Truck — Truck ID & Truck Wallet: On the road to truck autonomy

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Commercial Vehicles

## At a glance

An autonomous truck-wallet MVP for Daimler Truck — Bundesbank-authorized eMoney and distributed ledger on real truck hardware, enabling secure autonomous fuel payments.

## Initial situation

Global supply chains face a historical turnaround: resource shortages and the quest for higher efficiency and asset utilization create pressure toward autonomous trucks and related functionalities.

## Approach

In an iterative process we developed approaches toward an autonomously functioning truck wallet — starting from blockchain, pivoting to autonomous payments and interaction with third-party environments such as fuel stations, using original Daimler Truck hardware.

## Results

- **Automated payment roundtrip** realized using Bundesbank-authorized eMoney and distributed ledger
- **Integration of wallet architecture** in actual Daimler Truck hardware (CTP)
- MVP with **digital fuel card** and autonomous authorization & payment at fuel stations
- **Increased transaction security** and effective reduction of card fraud
