# DOLL Fahrzeugbau — Data and AI Platform Assessment

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Commercial Vehicles

## At a glance

A data & AI platform assessment for DOLL Fahrzeugbau — discovery, target architecture on MS Fabric and three prioritized MVP use cases to deliver value fast.

## Initial situation

DOLL faced major challenges from manual, fragmented processes across planning, sales and service. Data was scattered across systems with no central platform or cohesive data strategy, hindering cross-functional collaboration and slowing strategic decisions.

## Approach

We conducted a structured discovery phase to understand DOLL's key challenges and defined a clear strategic and technical foundation — shared purpose, vision and mission, target architecture and a phased roadmap with infrastructure setup and three high-impact use cases.

## Results

- Defined **Purpose, Vision, Mission statements** to support long-term direction
- Prioritized the **three most business-critical use cases as MVP** for implementation
- Created a **technical reference architecture** for a centralized data platform using MS Fabric
