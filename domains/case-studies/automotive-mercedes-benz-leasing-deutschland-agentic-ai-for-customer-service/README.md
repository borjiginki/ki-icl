# Mercedes-Benz Leasing Deutschland — Agentic AI for Customer Service

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Financial Services

## At a glance

A multi-agent, MCP-based AI assistant for MB Leasing Deutschland customer service — >30% lower handling time, +30% capacity and >20% more first-contact resolutions.

## Initial situation

Mercedes-Benz Leasing Deutschland faced a continuous increase in customer-service requests. Limited agent capacity and insufficient chatbot resolution led to long waiting times, frequent escalations, inconsistent experiences and growing pressure on service teams.

## Approach

We developed a multi-agent AI architecture providing real-time support for service employees. Leveraging Model Context Protocol (MCP), the AI agents integrate seamlessly into Mercedes' existing system landscape and connect to live data sources, with intelligent intent routing and answer-sourcing agents enabling faster, more accurate resolutions.

## Results

- Reduced **average ticket handling time by >30%**
- Resulting **capacity uplift of +30%** (from 46 to 60 calls per agent)
- Increased **first-contact resolutions by >20%**
- **MCP** ensures interoperability and a future-proof integration layer
- Foundation for **fully automated real-time customer interactions**
