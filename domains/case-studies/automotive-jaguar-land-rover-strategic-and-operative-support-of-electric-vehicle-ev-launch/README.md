# Jaguar Land Rover — Strategic and operative support of Electric Vehicle (EV) Launch

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Strategy

## At a glance

Strategic EV-launch support for Jaguar Land Rover's European HQ — market-diffusion and sales-volume models (90% accuracy) and balanced scorecards to right-size the retail network.

## Initial situation

As a traditional British provider of luxury sportscars and sedans, Jaguar was far from pioneering an EV launch and sought external expertise in launch support.

## Approach

We supported JLR's European HQ in developing their launch strategy, including diffusion models for selected markets and balanced scorecards to determine a right-sized retail network and the suitable retailers within it.

## Results

- Developed a **prediction model for market diffusion and sales volume** with **90% accuracy** for the own brand
- Identified **differentiated local EV market potentials**
- **Classified the retail network** by local potential and suitability
- Provided a **holistic model for future brand development**
