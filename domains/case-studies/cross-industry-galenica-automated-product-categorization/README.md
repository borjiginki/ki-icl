# Galenica — Automated Product Categorization

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Retail, E-Commerce & Omnichannel

## At a glance

An ML categorizer for Galenica that auto-classifies new products across five hierarchy levels with confidence scoring — cutting manual categorization by 87%.

## Initial situation

Galenica's web shop and pharmacies have a constantly changing portfolio with new products added daily. Each product must be categorized across five hierarchical levels — previously a manual, high-workload, error-prone process.

## Approach

We developed a machine learning solution that automatically categorizes new products by referencing the existing catalog. A confidence score facilitates focused manual validation, while AI-suggested categories offer insight into the current category structure.

## Results

- Manual categorizations **reduced by 87%**
- Average of **90 products** added/changed and categorized **daily**
- Categorizer matches against **several thousand categories** and product lines
