# Fressnapf — Reporting Factory

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Retail & Commerce

## At a glance

An end-to-end reporting setup for Fressnapf — from goal alignment and an MVP blueprint to a robust data platform and KPI-based performance tracking.

## Initial situation

Fressnapf aimed to build a holistic service ecosystem and explore new business models (IoT devices, insurance, grooming). Data and reporting were critical, but there was a gap between business needs and technical execution, unclear requirements and insufficiently defined processes.

## Approach

We applied a structured, end-to-end approach for a scalable, value-driven setup. After aligning on goals and roles, we assessed skills and reporting needs, implemented a first MVP use case as a blueprint, ensured a solid technical foundation and provided on-the-job enablement — then introduced performance tracking with clear KPIs.

## Results

- **Clear roadmap** and aligned actions for all stakeholders
- Defined **skill matrix and team setup** bridging business and tech
- **Robust technical architecture** for reporting and data platform
- Stakeholders enabled and **KPIs established** for sustainable adoption
