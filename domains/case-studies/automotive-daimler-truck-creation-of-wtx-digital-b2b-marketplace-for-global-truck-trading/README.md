# Daimler Truck — Creation of WTX — Digital B2B marketplace for global truck trading

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Commercial Vehicles

## At a glance

WTX — a global B2B marketplace for used trucks built from scratch for Daimler in under a year, with data-driven matchmaking and end-to-end logistics, payment and customs.

## Initial situation

Cross-border trade of used commercial vehicles was burdened by complexity and inefficiency — manual processes, fragmented payment systems and lack of transparency created delays, costs and fraud risk for dealers, logistics providers and buyers.

## Approach

We built a comprehensive global digital marketplace from the ground up, integrating intelligent matchmaking with end-to-end logistics and secure payment services for buyers and sellers. KI group owned market assessment, platform development, marketing, business development, operations set-up and fundraising.

## Results

- **Full-fledged marketplace** created from scratch and launched **within 1 year**
- International **end-to-end operations** established for full compliance and reliability
- Maximum efficiency through **data-driven matchmaking**, logistics and financing
- **Seamless end-to-end** fulfilment incl. shipping, export customs, payment and insurance
