# Dussmann — Early Warning System for Plant Health

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Facility Management

## At a glance

An IoT-driven predictive early warning system for one of Europe's largest vertical gardens, forecasting risks and reducing plant loss and manual monitoring.

## Initial situation

IoT sensors deployed in one of Europe's largest vertical gardens were analyzed to help maintenance teams proactively manage environmental conditions — detecting unfavorable changes before plant health deteriorates, reducing plant loss, replacement costs and manual monitoring.

## Approach

We designed and implemented data pipelines and predictive algorithms to monitor environmental parameters. By collecting and processing IoT sensor data we trained models to forecast environmental changes, enabling an intelligent early warning system that alerts staff when thresholds are at risk of being exceeded.

## Results

- **Predictive models** leveraging IoT sensor data
- Real-time **early warning system** for plant health monitoring
- **Proactive optimization** of environmental conditions, reducing manual intervention
