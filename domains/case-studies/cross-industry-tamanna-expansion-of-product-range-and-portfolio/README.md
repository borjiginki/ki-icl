# Tamanna — Expansion of Product Range and Portfolio

**Sector:** Cross-Industry · **Industry:** Retail, E-Commerce & Omnichannel

## At a glance

A flexible digital infrastructure that consolidated product data, enabling Tamanna to scale brands and categories rapidly across KSA and Kuwait.

## Initial situation

Tamanna, an e-commerce giant operating in KSA and Kuwait, had ambitious plans to expand its fashion and beauty product line digitally — onboarding more brands and broadening product categories.

## Approach

Our solution enabled a flexible digital landscape that adapts easily to market demands. Reordering categories and new catalogue additions could be done seamlessly thanks to the consolidation of all available data.

## Results

- **> 1 million USD** in transaction volume enabled
- System for **early detection and resolution** of data quality issues
- Expansion of product range with **ensured data accuracy**
