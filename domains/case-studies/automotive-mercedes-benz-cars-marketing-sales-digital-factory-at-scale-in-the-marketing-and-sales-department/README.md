# Mercedes-Benz Cars Marketing & Sales — Digital Factory at scale in the Marketing and Sales department

**Sector:** Automotive · **Industry:** Automotive · Sales

## At a glance

A 'Digital Factory' automating Marketing & Sales admin processes at Mercedes-Benz — a delivery pipeline, 900 validated savings initiatives and ~200 people enabled.

## Initial situation

Within the client's Marketing and Sales department, a reduction of 50 FTEs through digitalization and automation was targeted, requiring automation of manual administrative processes without disrupting remaining team members' workflows.

## Approach

We transformed the workflow, establishing a continuous prioritized delivery pipeline, optimizing data flow and introducing data-analysis tools for standardized analyses. Our modular development approach ensured seamless integration and smooth onboarding across the business.

## Results

- Validation of **900 potential savings** initiatives
- Clear **roles, responsibilities, working documents and processes** established
- **Enablement** of business and IT (~200 trained employees)
- **Full automation** of various previously manual processes
