# The AI-Native Company

**The hook:** *"We make you an AI-native company — by building your company brain."*

**What we actually sell:** *"We make you an AI-native company — we enable your people, refound your processes, and build your company brain."*

## The idea in one breath

Intelligence is rented and identical for everyone — every competitor can buy the same models. What decides impact is **context**: what an organization knows, captured in a form that software and agents can act on. That is the one factor a company controls, and it is buildable. We build it as the **company brain** — a governed, self-improving context layer over the systems the organization already runs — and we refound processes onto it while enabling the client's own people to keep going without us.

Most organizations' AI response today is a chatbot or copilot rollout: the first two rungs of a six-rung ladder, delivering a few percent while looking like a strategy. We sell the jump from single-player tool use to an operating model.

## Three strands, braided

| Strand | What we do | Proof it worked |
|---|---|---|
| **People** | Enable a cohort of the client's own people — functional, no coding assumed — to build for themselves. | A non-engineer has shipped a reusable agent. |
| **Processes** | Rethink each process around three actors — humans, software, agents — and remodel its artifacts. Process by process, never big-bang. | The process runs as a closed loop in production, with a named human owner signing off quality. |
| **Brain** | Build the governed, write-back context layer over their systems of record, with agent identity, audit, and a nightly learning loop. | The system compounds without us. |

None of the three works alone: a brain without redesign is a faster nervous system for the old company; enablement without the brain leaves fifty private setups that drift apart and walk out the door; process redesign without the other two is a recommendation deck that peaks on handover day.

## How it is bought

Four fixed-price modules on a five-gate arc — **Discover · Lighthouse · Champion enablement · Foundation**. Every gate is a real decision point: continue, pause, or stop. The next module's scope and price are fixed at the previous gate, on evidence. Entry is the discovery module, measured in days.
