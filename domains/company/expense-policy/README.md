## What is reimbursed

KI group reimburses costs that are necessary to do the work and that would not have been incurred otherwise.

- **Travel.** Rail second class, economy air, and the cheapest fare booked at least seven days ahead where the schedule allows.
- **Accommodation.** A single room at a business-standard hotel.
- **Meals.** Actual cost while travelling on company business, within the per-day cap below.
- **Client hospitality.** Only with prior written agreement from the engagement lead.

## What is not reimbursed

- Commuting between home and a KI group office.
- Fines, penalties, and traffic charges.
- Personal items bought during a business trip.
- Upgrades of any kind, unless a medical need is documented in advance.

## Receipt rules

Every claim needs an original receipt.
A card statement is not a receipt, because it does not show what was bought.

- Submit receipts within 30 days of the expense date.
- A photograph is acceptable if every line is legible.
- Foreign-currency receipts are converted at the card issuer's rate on the transaction date. Attach the statement line as evidence of that rate.
- A missing receipt below 25 EUR can be replaced by a written declaration countersigned by your lead. Above 25 EUR there is no substitute.

## Approval thresholds

| Amount | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to 250 EUR | Your team lead |
| 250 to 1,000 EUR | Department head |
| Above 1,000 EUR | Managing director |

Approval must be obtained before the cost is incurred whenever the amount is known in advance.
A retrospective approval request is a defensible exception, not the normal route.

## Timing

Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll run, provided they are submitted by the 20th.
